# 📅 Calendar & Meeting Management System

## Executive Calendar Management Portfolio Project

A practical executive support system designed to organize a CEO's schedule, protect focus time, coordinate meetings, and improve meeting preparation.

This project was developed as part of an Executive Operations & Calendar Support capstone for a fast-growing technology startup.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal was to build a professional executive support system that:

- Reduces scheduling conflicts
- Organizes the CEO's 90-day schedule
- Uses time-blocking to protect productive work periods
- Coordinates internal and external meetings
- Standardizes meeting preparation and documentation
- Creates systems that can be easily followed by a future Executive Assistant

---

## 📅 Calendar Management

The project included a 90-day CEO calendar built around structured time blocks.

### Key calendar blocks

- Morning Routine & Exercise
- Review Emails & Daily Priorities
- Deep Work
- Team Meetings
- Product Development Review
- Lunch Break
- Investor/Partner Meetings
- Staff Supervision
- Email Follow-up
- Review Tomorrow's Schedule

The schedule was designed to balance strategic meetings, operational responsibilities, communication, and protected focus time.

---

## 🤝 Meeting Coordination

Sample meetings included:

- Executive Strategy Sync
- Investor Funding Progress Call
- Product Sprint Review
- Beta Launch Preparation
- Internal Strategy Meeting
- Partnership Roundtable
- Mobile App Beta Demo

Meetings were scheduled with clear dates, times, descriptions, and meeting locations where applicable.

### Meeting Agenda Template

The meeting agenda structure includes:

- Meeting title

- Participants
- Meeting objective

- Decisions required
- Action items
- Next steps

### Meeting Minutes Template

The minutes structure captures:

- Meeting details
- Attendees
- Key discussion points
- Decisions made
- Assigned action items
- Owners
- Deadlines
- Follow-up requirements

---

## ⏰ Time-Blocking Strategy

The calendar uses time-blocking to prevent the CEO's schedule from becoming completely meeting-driven.

Protected periods include:

- Deep-work blocks
- Lunch breaks
- Administrative follow-up
- Schedule review
- Strategic work periods

Buffer time was also incorporated into the broader 90-day operational sprint.

---

## 🔄 Daily Executive Support Workflow

### Morning

1. Review and triage incoming communication.
2. Confirm the CEO's daily agenda.
3. Verify meeting links and locations.
4. Identify priority meetings and preparation requirements.

### During the Day

1. Protect deep-work and focus blocks.
2. Monitor schedule changes.
3. Coordinate meetings and follow-ups.
4. Track action items.

### End of Day

1. Update task statuses.
2. Review outstanding follow-ups.
3. Check the following day's calendar.
4. Prepare the next-day briefing.
